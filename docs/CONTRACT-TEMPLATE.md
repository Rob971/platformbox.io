# PlatformBox — Delivery Terms (DRAFT)

> **DRAFT — review by legal counsel before use.**
> This template captures the commercial protections described on
> platformbox.io. It is not a complete Master Services Agreement; merge it
> with your standard MSA or Statement of Work.

## A. Definitions

- **"Working Day"** means any day other than Saturday, Sunday, or a public
  holiday in Spain, including public holidays observed in Barcelona
  (Catalonia).
- **"Delivery Period"** means fourteen (14) Working Days.
- **"Kickoff Date"** means the date on which all Start Conditions in
  Clause B are satisfied.
- **"Platform"** means the deliverables defined in the Statement of Work.
- **"Client"** means the customer; **"Provider"** means PlatformBox.

## B. Start Conditions (when the clock starts)

The Delivery Period begins on the Kickoff Date. The Kickoff Date occurs only
when **all** of the following are satisfied:

1. This Agreement is signed by both parties.
2. The initial payment has cleared.
3. The Client has granted the agreed access credentials to its AWS account
   and source repositories.
4. The Platform Assessment / discovery has been completed and the Statement
   of Work signed off by the Client.
5. The Client has named a single point of contact with authority to make
   decisions.

Any days between signing and satisfaction of these conditions are **not**
counted against the Delivery Period.

## C. Client obligations and dependencies

The Client shall:

- provide access and information within **three (3) Working Days** of a
  written request;
- respond to decision requests within **two (2) Working Days**;
- sign off on milestone deliverables within **two (2) Working Days** of
  receipt.

## D. Extension for client-caused delay

If the Client fails to meet any obligation in Clause C, or otherwise delays
the work (including, without limitation, failure to grant access,
unavailability of the point of contact, or a request to pause work), the
Delivery Period is **extended day-for-day** by the documented duration of
the delay, with no liability, penalty, or refund owed by the Provider. The
Provider shall notify the Client in writing when such a delay occurs.

## E. Change control

Any change to the agreed scope requested after the Kickoff Date shall be
agreed in writing and may adjust the Delivery Period and/or price. No change
is effective until documented.

## F. Acceptance (definition of done)

The Platform is "delivered" when it meets the acceptance criteria in the
Statement of Work. Upon notice of delivery, the Client has **five (5)
Working Days** to test and notify the Provider in writing of any specific,
material defects. If the Client does not deliver a written list of defects
within that window, the Platform is **deemed accepted**. The Provider shall
correct notified defects; re-testing is limited to the scope of those
defects and does not reopen the full acceptance window.

## G. Force majeure

Neither party is liable for delay caused by events beyond its reasonable
control (for example, third-party cloud provider outages). Such events
extend the Delivery Period by the duration of the event.

## H. Payment

Fees are fixed-price for the defined scope, not time-based. Payment
milestones are tied to **delivery**, not calendar dates, and the final
payment falls due upon delivery (not upon the Client's subjective
satisfaction), subject to Clause F.

## I. Governing law and disputes

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Spain. The parties shall first
attempt good-faith negotiation for thirty (30) days; thereafter, disputes
shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Barcelona.
